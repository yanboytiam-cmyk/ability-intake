Before you begin

This replaces the paper admission packet. It takes about 15 minutes, and it covers every form the clinic needs: both consents to treatment, the intake questionnaire, your emergency contacts, HIPAA, telehealth, and information sharing.

You only enter each answer once. Your name, date of birth, address and insurance are carried across every form, and you sign a single time at the end.

Have these ready: your photo ID and your insurance card. You will photograph the front and back of each.

Your answers are saved as you go. Close the page and pick up where you stopped.
